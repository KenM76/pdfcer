# lab-ink — provenance and attribution

Two single-page PDF fixtures pinning that **a `Lab` colour on a page that
composites in ink separates through the document's output intent** — the PCS
route of `Pass 242.0` — on all three routes a colour can take: a fill, a direct
sampled image, and an `/Indexed` image. Generated by
`tools/gen-lab-ink-fixtures.py`; used by `crates/pdfcer-render/tests/lab_ink.rs`.

The `Lab` counterpart of `fixtures/synthetic/icc-rgb/` (a space WITH a profile)
and `fixtures/synthetic/devicen-image/` (a space WITH colorants). `Lab` has
neither, which is why it needed a route of its own.

## The files

| file | page group | `/OutputIntents` | what it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lab-three-ways-subtractive.pdf` | `/CS /DeviceCMYK` | `dest-cmyk.icc` (see below) | the PCS route: all three objects separate through the intent's B2A table |
| `lab-three-ways-subtractive-no-intent.pdf` | `/CS /DeviceCMYK` | none | the control: no PCS route exists, all three bridge through `rgb_to_cmyk` |

The two differ ONLY in the `/OutputIntents` entry. Each draws `Lab (60, 0, 0)`
— the print-conformance patch's own grey — in three 60 × 60 pt boxes at
x = 10, 80, 150 on a 230 × 100 pt page: fill (`60 0 0 scn`), direct 8-bit
image (bytes `153 128 128`, exactly `L* 60, a* 0, b* 0` under the default
decode and `/Range [-128 127 -128 127]`), and an `/Indexed` image with that
one palette entry.

## Why a neutral, and why it discriminates

A press profile separates a neutral into all four inks (grey component
replacement); `rgb_to_cmyk` separates it into K alone. On the intent page the
probed ink is `(0.24, 0.24, 0.24, 0.16)`; on the control it is
`(0, 0, 0, 0.43)`. The two candidate answers are far apart in C, M and Y
(standing rule R225), and the test also asserts the intent CHANGED the ink,
which `rgb_to_cmyk` — which never reads an output intent — cannot do.

## What the fixture found on the day it was written

The `/Indexed` image painted near-black on BOTH pages beside two routes that
agreed: `image::palette_entry` divided every palette byte by 255, so an
`/Indexed` over `Lab` decoded `L* 60` as `L* 0.6`. The fill path's
`indexed_to_rgb` had scaled into the base's `component_range` all along.
Fixed in the same Pass; the control page pins it.

## Not covered here, and why

The D50 white-point adaptation in `ColorSpace::to_pcs_xyz` is the identity on
these fixtures (their `Lab` declares D50), so a missing adaptation passes them
— found by sabotage. It is pinned by a unit test on a D65-declared `Lab`
(`color::tests::to_pcs_xyz_adapts_the_declared_white_to_d50`), not by a
fixture.

## `dest-cmyk.icc` — attribution

The intent page embeds `fixtures/synthetic/icc-rgb/dest-cmyk.icc` byte for
byte: iccce's `v2-cmyk-chromatic-neutral.icc` (`github.com/KenM76/iccce`,
MIT, same author), whose B2A separates a neutral into C = M = Y and K. See
`fixtures/synthetic/icc-rgb/PROVENANCE.md` for the full record. Rights-cleared
per `docs/LEGAL.md` §5.
